# Support

I maintain Ghostlight on my own. Ask in public whenever you can, and expect best-effort timing:
there are no separate support agreements, because there is nothing to buy.

Use the narrowest lane that fits:

- Reproducible defects and installation friction: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/sylin-org/ghostlight/issues).
- Questions, ideas, feature requests, policy patterns, and show-and-tell: [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/sylin-org/ghostlight/discussions).
- Suspected vulnerabilities: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and report privately.
- Licensing, or anything that should not be public: email hello@sylin.org.

Before you share diagnostics, strip credentials, cookies, private page content, opaque identities,
and personal paths. Ghostlight's audit trail and workbench history hold no payloads, but they still
describe which tools ran, what was decided, and when. Open **Status** in the workbench for service,
browser, authority, and notification health, and **MCP integrations** to see exactly which clients
Ghostlight is registered with.

[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) covers how requests are evaluated and how to prepare a code
contribution. [MAINTENANCE.md](MAINTENANCE.md) is where I write down what depends on me alone, and
what happens if I step away.
