Preserve Ghostlight tabs

Keep controlled pages open as visible evidence after Ghostlight works. When enabled, agent requests to close tabs are refused even when service policy permits them. You can always close tabs yourself.

Agent activity effects

The phantom cursor, click ripples, scan-lines, and the rest while the agent works. Turn off for a completely quiet browser.

Action captions

A subtitle naming each action ("Reading page", "Screenshot"). Handy when recording a demo; off for everyday.

Developer diagnostics

Keeps bounded connection lifecycle details available in the extension while diagnostics are enabled. Off by default.

Governance and policy

Which domains the agent may touch, capability limits, tab-close authority, and the audit log are configured in the Ghostlight binary you installed -- not here. This extension holds no policy. Its preserve-tabs choice is a local physical interlock that can refuse closing but never grant authority.
