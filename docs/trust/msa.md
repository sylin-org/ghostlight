# Ghostlight Master Software Agreement (Retired)

> **RETIRED 2026-08-25 by [ADR-0140](../adr/0140-fully-open-source-licensing.md).** Ghostlight
> no longer offers paid licensing, so there is no commercial agreement to paper. This page
> previously held a draft master software agreement template for governance-module licensees;
> the exact former text remains recoverable from git history at this path.

What replaced it:

- **License:** the whole product, including the governance module, is Apache-2.0 OR MIT. The
  license itself grants everything a customer agreement used to: use, modification,
  redistribution, and operational deployment at any organization size. See
  [LICENSING.md](../../LICENSING.md) and the [licensing guide](../guides/licensing.md).
- **Continuity:** the Continuity Promise -- no activation, no behavior gate, no phone-home --
  was never contractual to begin with. It is a property of the shipped software, recorded in
  [ADR-0028](../adr/0028-tripwire-licensing-and-continuity-promise.md) Decision 6 and evidenced in
  [continuity.md](continuity.md).
- **Data flows:** the fact this agreement's privacy section once summarized is documented
  directly in [data-flows.md](data-flows.md): the runtime executes entirely on your
  infrastructure and no data flows to the vendor.
- **Support:** community support with best-effort response; see [support-policy.md](support-policy.md).
